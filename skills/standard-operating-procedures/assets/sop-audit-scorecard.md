# Standard operating procedure audit scorecard

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. A high score does not replace domain, safety, privacy, or compliance review.

Audit date: [Date]
Auditor: [Name]

| Quality controls are observable | [0, 1, 2] | [evidence] | [gap] | [repair] |
