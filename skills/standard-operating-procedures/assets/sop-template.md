# Standard operating procedure template

Write the procedure so a trained reader can perform it consistently and know when to stop or escalate. Use visible gaps when the described process is incomplete.

```text
Procedure: [Name]
Procedure owner: [Name or Owner needed]
Version: [Version]
Effective date: [Date or Date needed]
Next review date: [Date or Date needed]
Approved by: [Name or Approver needed]

Purpose
[Outcome this procedure exists to produce]

Scope
Applies to: [People, work, systems, or locations]
Does not apply to: [Exclusions]

Trigger and completion
Starts when: [Observable trigger]
Ends when: [Observable completion condition]

Roles
| Role | Responsibility | Decision authority | Backup |
|---|---|---|---|
| [role] | [responsibility] | [decision] | [role or Backup needed] |

Prerequisites and inputs
- Required access or training: [List]
- Required information or materials: [List]
- Safety, privacy, compliance, or approval checks: [List or Expert review needed]

Procedure
| Step | Owner | Action | Input | Output or evidence | Due or service target | If this fails |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [role] | [one observable action] | [input] | [record or result] | [time or Not specified] | [retry, stop, or escalate] |

Decision points
| Decision | Condition | Yes path | No path | Decider |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [question] | [evidence used] | [step] | [step] | [role] |

Exceptions and escalation
| Condition | Immediate response | Escalation owner | Response date or target | Required record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [exception] | [response] | [Name or Owner needed] | [Date or target needed] | [record] |

Quality and controls
| Check | Standard | Checker | Evidence | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [check] | [observable bar] | [role] | [record] | [frequency] |

Records and retention
- Record: [What is created]
- Location: [Approved location or Location needed]
- Retention rule: [Supplied rule or Policy needed]

Change history
| Version | Date | Change | Author | Approver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [version] | [date] | [change] | [name] | [name] |

Open gaps and actions
- [Gap]. Owner: [Name or Owner needed]. Due: [Date or Date needed].
```
